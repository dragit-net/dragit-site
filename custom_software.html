Portal — Active Product

Operational layer above your ERP

Portal is a hybrid web/mobile application that sits on top of your ERP — KPI dashboards, alarms, CRUD records, production work orders, web pivot reporting. In production at Boksit, ArcelorMittal, Soligna and others.

KPI Dashboards

Live operational metrics across production, logistics, and finance — no manual exports, no waiting on a report.

Alarms & Notifications

Threshold-based alerts for batch jobs, integration failures, and KPI breaches — you hear it before your customer does.

CRUD & Work Orders

Mobile-first CRUD records, customer orders, work orders, and daily production records — the shop floor stops phoning the office.

ERP Integration

Native integration with D365 F&O, AX2012, and custom ERP via OData / REST / BizTalk — one source of truth, no duplicate data entry.
