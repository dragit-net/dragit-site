What we are writing about

Six articles, written in English and Serbian in parallel. None of them is published yet — until an article is finished, this page stays out of the search index.

  • AX 2012 to Dynamics 365 F&O: what migration really involves (and where teams go wrong). Fit-gap analysis, data migration effort, and the upgrade traps that only surface after go-live.
  • The end of Microsoft Dynamics GP support: why the next few years are a window for cloud ERP. What the lifecycle change actually forces you to decide, and how to start without a big-bang project.
  • Mission Control: see your D365 F&O in real time. Batch jobs, integration pipelines and order flow in one view — built on Azure Data Explorer, Log Analytics and Application Insights.
  • ERP delivery first-hand: what I learned building and delivering an ERP. The estimation errors, the requirements that changed, and the decisions that paid off across 25+ years of implementations.
  • Agentic automation (n8n) in manufacturing: where to start, and where not to. Which processes are safe to automate first, and which ones need a governed design first.
  • A portal as an extension of your ERP: the integrations that actually move numbers. ERP, MES, CRM and BI connected in one place — and what that does to daily operations.
