Before you reach out

Frequently asked

The six questions every D365 / ERP buyer asks before the first call.

What does the 30-minute discovery call involve?

We look at your D365 F&O or ERP environment together. I walk you through where the blind spots are — batch jobs, integrations, access, monitoring — and tell you what I'd do about them. No prep, no sales deck, no obligation. Most calls end with two or three concrete next steps.

Do you work with teams outside the region (EU / US)?

Yes. My anchor reference is Ragn-Sells in Sweden — a full D365 F&O implementation plus a live real-time monitoring layer across international teams, running in production since 2021. I work in English, in the EU time zone, and hand off to partner integrators (e.g. Daxact) where a delivery team is required.

How do you handle Microsoft's biannual D365 F&O updates?

The core of the practice is upgrade-safe architecture: light, standard-first customization so that a platform update doesn't break custom code. Mission Control exists precisely to see the effect of an update on batch and integrations before your users do.

What is included in Mission Control (observability)?

Five business dashboards across UAT / TRAIN / PROD built on Azure App Insights, Log Analytics, Azure Data Explorer and KQL: order / load flow, NVV process, batch jobs, DMF / BizTalk / TCS integrations, and an IT overview — 20+ external systems wired in.

Can you work alongside our existing integrator or partner?

That's the usual shape. I bring architecture, delivery coordination and the monitoring layer; a local integrator (Daxact, M&I Systems) carries the build team. One accountable owner on architecture — no layers between you and the person who designed it.

What does "on time, on budget" mean in practice?

It means a fixed, transparent scope with a named owner and a published definition of done — 19 projects have held that line. When a milestone is at risk you hear it from me, not from a status report, with a recommended path before the date, not after.
