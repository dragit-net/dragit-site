How a project runs

Three steps, one team

Every engagement runs in three steps. The architect who leads the build stays with the system after go-live, so nothing is handed over to a team that was not in the room.

1. Discovery & Proposal

We start from how your business runs today, not from a feature list. Interviews with the people doing the work, plus a look at the data, show where effort gets repeated, where a cycle takes longer than it should, and what is entered twice.

You get a written proposal: what D365 F&O covers as standard, where customization is genuinely needed, in which order we build, and what it costs.

2. Planning & Execution

The plan becomes a working system. Configuration, extensions and integrations are built on a UAT environment, with test scenarios written together with your key users rather than handed to them at the end.

Monitoring is designed in the same breath as the build, not bolted on afterwards, so the dashboards exist from the first release.

3. Delivery & Support

Before go-live we rehearse the cutover on the test environment, train the users, and agree who owns what in the first weeks of running it.

After that we maintain the system and tune it against the data you read every day, not against month-end reports.

Migrations and legacy replacements run through the same three steps — see them in practice.
